What this model was trained on

This model was adapted on original-language biblical text from two named editions, using a development split:

Hebrew & AramaicOSHB / WLC

The Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible’s Westminster Leningrad Codex text.

Greek New TestamentSBLGNT

The Society of Biblical Literature Greek New Testament, a modern edited text.

These editions are a starting point. They do not recover the earliest wording of every passage, and the model has not been adapted on the full range of ancient manuscripts and versions. This research preview can make translation and interpretation mistakes.
