About this private beta

Meforash uses a model fine-tuned on selected Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek biblical editions. It can make mistakes; its training does not establish a single recovered original text.

Your invitation credentials are sent only to this beta to start a secure session.

About, privacy & sources

During email sign-in, this page may keep the visible conversation, source cards, and unsent draft in this tab's temporary session storage for up to ten minutes so a refresh does not erase them. That copy does not contain your email address or sign-in code, and clears after recovery, cancellation, a new conversation, or sign-out.
