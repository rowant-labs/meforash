About this private beta

Meforash uses a model fine-tuned on selected Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek biblical editions. It can make mistakes; its training does not establish a single recovered original text.

Your invitation credentials are sent only to this beta to start a secure session.

Your questions and conversation are sent to Tinker only when you ask for an answer. Conversation text is kept in memory for the session, is not written to disk by this preview, and is not used by Meforash for training.

Passage cards use selected scholarly editions of Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts. These editions are not the original manuscripts, and the cards do not independently verify every claim in an answer.
