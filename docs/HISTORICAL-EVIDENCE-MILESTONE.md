## Record and review contract

The manifest's draft JSON Schema separates:

- **Sources and citations:** stable work/witness/object ID, edition or translator, precise verse/page/line locator, public URL, access date, immutable source version and content hash. A live branch URL is a discovery link until its artifact is pinned.
- **Readings:** exact original representation, independently labeled English rendering, witness type and hand, and whether text is present, absent, damaged, supplied, uncertain, unpreserved or unchecked. A modern edition and an ancient version are not two additional manuscript votes.
- **Dates:** narrated setting, proposed composition, physical witness and modern edition each get their own ranges, BCE/CE convention, dating method, citation and uncertainty. Unknown dates remain unknown.
- **Claims:** distinguish observation, edited reading, translation, historical inference, later interpretation and contemporary reflection. Attach supporting and contrary evidence, defensible alternatives and coverage limits. Disagreement is attributed; it is not resolved by counting editions or asking an LLM for consensus.
- **Use decisions:** research access, app display, redistribution, training and adapter release are separate fields for each component. All new training eligibility begins as pending. A reviewed original summary is not automatically free of its source's obligations.

Proposed workflow: an author drafts from identified evidence; a different relevant language/textual specialist checks every reading, claim and substantial inference; a second specialist adjudicates contested cases. Use Hebrew/Aramaic and Greek expertise, plus period expertise for historical notes. Record qualifications, interpretive perspective, conflicts and unresolved disagreement. No specialist has been engaged for this milestone. AI checks may help find broken citations or omissions but do not substitute for that review.

Completion requires all 40 records to pass schema, locator/hash and rights checks; every publishable assertion to have evidence or an explicit inference label; zero unsupported witness-attestation claims; and separate author/reviewer approval. If a proposed item remains blocked, retain its coverage-gap record and replace it transparently with a sourced item in the same allocation. Do not fill missing manuscripts, dates or scholarly conclusions with generated guesses.

## How this feeds the trained assistant

At answer time, the private B chat should retrieve the relevant approved passage, dossier or note, expose citations, and acknowledge coverage gaps. Exact quotation can be checked mechanically; whether evidence supports an interpretation still needs evaluation.

A future training proposal can test **evidence handling**: distinguish witness from edition, preserve a negation, explain alternative readings, cite the supplied evidence and decline unsupported reconstruction. Construct targets from approved records, permit multiple defensible renderings, and include incomplete-evidence cases. A separately versioned source-corpus adaptation could later add eligible witness/context text with explicit source labels. Do not collapse alternatives into a model-invented original or use unreviewed model answers as authority.

Before training or prompt tuning, separate evaluators freeze new questions, source-backed criteria and exclusions without showing them to training authors. Compare unchanged A and retained fine-tuned B under the same evidence access, then any new adapter. Test model memory, interpretation with a correct supplied packet, and actual lookup separately; include broad English questions, follow-ups and intent-appropriate life reflection. This distinguishes missing evidence, lookup failure and model misunderstanding. The present plan authorizes none of those paid runs or external commissions; it makes the next collection concrete and reviewable.
