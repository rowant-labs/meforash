## Uses that do not require permission

You may use the word **Meforash** accurately and in plain text to:

- identify or link to the official project or service;
- describe the project's code, model, methods, results, or history;
- review, discuss, teach about, criticize, or report on Meforash;
- state truthfully that software is compatible with, uses, connects to, or was derived from Meforash; or
- provide the attribution or notices required by an applicable license.

Make the relationship clear. A modified distribution may say, for example, “Derived from the Meforash open-source project” if that statement is accurate and the distribution is also clearly labeled as an independent, unofficial version.

You may exercise all rights provided by the applicable open-source licenses. If you distribute or operate a modified version:

These requirements concern branding and source identification. They do not prevent accurate notices that the software includes or derives from Apache-licensed Meforash code.
