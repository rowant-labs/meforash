## Inputs the next runner must bind

The runner must consume a use-specific evidence projection, its registry and the underlying record/source hashes. A projection alone is insufficient: validate its provenance and current dependencies, and reject missing, changed, non-approved or wrong-use content. For model-input packets, the recorded use basis must cover supplying that content to the inference provider; approval to redistribute or train does not substitute for that decision. A checked registry records review decisions; it does not authenticate reviewer qualifications or confer rights.

Freeze these artifacts before candidate generation:

- Questions, criteria and exposure/exclusion inventory, with separate authorship and disclosure records.
- Evidence selection for each case, including source locators, alternatives and limitations; no expected answers or review instructions in model packets.
- Registry, selected-use projection, source bytes, prompt, lookup policy and renderer/code hashes.
- B checkpoint identity, native tokenizer/template, settings, request order, stop rules and cost bound; keep provider locations private.
- Complete case-by-condition slot inventory and masked review/integration method.

The first eight evidence topics and their follow-ups remain development material. Their source improvements do not make reworded questions a fresh generalization test. Distinguish exposure to the biblical base corpus from exposure to the evaluation question or its specific textual issue.

## Offline acceptance cases

Implement the new runner using constructed fixtures before authoring or revealing actual questions. Its dry run must prove these behaviors without importing credentials or contacting a model:

| Constructed case | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Empty eligible projection | Produce a readiness report identifying the missing evidence. Do not silently construct a memory-only substitute for the evidence experiment. |
| Altered source, component, registry or projection | Reject the stale binding before any submission. |
| Approved training use, pending model-input/app use | Exclude the component from inference evidence. |
| Pending or rejected contrary evidence needed by a claim | Exclude the incomplete claim package; do not drop its contrary evidence to make it eligible. |
| Packet and lookup differ | Record both actual inputs and their hashes independently, including an empty lookup result. |
| One case across three core conditions | Produce exactly three distinct planned slots with the same question and declared settings. |
| Deadline failure after submission | Preserve an uncertain outcome, stop as specified, and do not resubmit automatically. |
| Output-limit response with partial final content | Preserve and review the final content; count it separately from a complete answer. |
| Unsubmitted slots after a stop | Retain them in the denominator inventory without inventing model failures or answers. |
| Review before label disclosure | Bind judgments to response hashes and freeze them before loading the arm key. |

Use the existing native diagnostics and prior versioned runners as references; do not modify or reuse their frozen experiment settings as the new protocol. The proposed core design remains B-memory, B-packet and B-lookup on thirty cases, or ninety planned requests. That allocation is still a proposal. Do not represent an unimplemented lookup policy as an available treatment.

## Completion belongs in the decision

The final protocol must predeclare both an answer-quality gate and a collection-completeness gate. Comparing only matched completed answers can hide failures or output limits in an evidence condition. Report completion across all planned slots, critical errors across all returned reviewable content, and omissions/preferences on their explicit matched denominators. If collection is too incomplete for the predeclared gate, the result is inconclusive; a favorable partial preference count does not promote the feature.

Choose the exact completeness threshold, severity rubric and any contingency before generation. Keep engineering fixture results, development-topic comparisons and fresh generalization results separate. A small AI-reviewed comparison supports a bounded development decision, not a whole-Bible accuracy claim.

## Ready to execute means

Actual eligible material supports the selected cases; questions and criteria have been checked; the protocol and dry-run slot inventory are frozen; the retained checkpoint is verified; current provider pricing and the concrete execution allowance are recorded. The completed known-topic pilot met these conditions only for its narrow inventory. A future broader or fresh-source run must satisfy them again; this document confers no continuing execution authority.
